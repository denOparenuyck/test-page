Concierge Services

We provide concierge-level recovery services tailored to each individual and family, meeting clients where they are and guiding them through every stage of the recovery process. Support is individualized and may include case management, recovery coaching, intervention services, and safe transport, depending on each client’s needs. Our team works closely with families and professionals to deliver structured yet flexible care, offering consistent guidance, accountability, and compassionate support focused on long-term stability and recovery.

Based on each family’s needs and support system, we can provide case management, recovery coaching, intervention and safe transport services.
Coordination

Case Management

Every plan is built around the unique needs, goals, and lifestyle of each client. Our Admissions Team works closely with clients and their families to develop a personalized Case Management plan that provides the right balance of structure, accountability, and flexibility — whether that means daily check-ins, weekly meetings, or a customized combination of support services. Each client is paired with a dedicated Case Manager who provides ongoing guidance, coordination, and compassionate support while helping navigate real-world challenges such as relapse prevention, legal matters, employment, and education.

Care & Structure

Intervention/ Safe Transport

A structured, compassionate process designed to help individuals take the first step toward recovery while providing support and guidance for the entire family. Our team works closely with you and your loved one to develop a thoughtful plan tailored to your specific circumstances, helping navigate difficult conversations with clarity, care, and purpose. From preparation and communication to ongoing follow-through, we ensure that every stage of the intervention is handled safely and professionally. Depending on your unique needs, we carefully match you with the right specialist to guide the process, offering the structure, accountability, and support necessary to encourage meaningful change and create a pathway toward lasting recovery.
