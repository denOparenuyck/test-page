Who We Are

Built by People Who’ve Lived It

Release is a nationally recognized behavioral healthcare community offering safe, private, and confidential support for substance use and mental health across New York and Texas. Founded by Zac Clark, a leader with lived experience in long-term recovery, the organization is committed to breaking the stigma surrounding addiction and mental health nationwide.

Since our start in 2017, our outcomes speak for themselves. We’ve witnessed hundreds of stories of recovery. We know this work changes lives, not just for the people we treat but the communities that surround them. Our team is a strong mix of licensed clinicians, experienced operators and individuals who have gone through their own struggles and come out on the other side.  

We combine clinical expertise with real-world understanding, strong community, and an environment built for sustainable change. Whether you’re seeking support for yourself or someone you love, you’ll find a team that listens, understands, and shows up.

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Our Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP) provides intensive, structured treatment, creating a balanced and flexible environment for those on their path to recovery. This program is ideal for clients who need more support than outpatient services can offer, but do not require 24/7 residential care.

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Our Intensive Outpatient Program provides structured clinical care in a flexible format. The 12-week curriculum covers emotional regulation, self-awareness, relapse prevention, boundaries, and other essential tools for recovery and daily life.

Outpatient Program (OP)

Our outpatient program is a step down from IOP. It includes fewer hours but maintains key weekly touchpoints like individual therapy, group therapy, medication management, and family support. This option is best for clients who are ready for more independence while staying connected to care.

About

Release was founded in 2017 by Zac Clark with one goal in mind: help as many people experience the gift of recovery that he was granted.

What began as a personal mission has grown into a full-spectrum recovery community built on compassion, clinical expertise, and lived experience. Our evidence-based approach supports individuals and families through every step of the healing process.

Story

Our Journey

2017
We opened the doors to our first transitional living residence in Westchester County — the beginning of a mission dedicated to helping individuals rebuild their lives through recovery, support, and community.
2018
We began building a team rooted in lived experience, compassion, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to hope and healing.
2019
Identifying the growing need for safe and supportive recovery environments for women, we launched our women’s program in New York City. Our founder, Zac Clark, recognized that financial barriers were preventing many individuals from accessing treatment and established the Release Foundation to provide scholarships for those unable to afford the care they deserve.
2020
We opened our first men’s transitional living residence in Manhattan just before the onset of COVID-19. Even during unprecedented challenges, we remained committed to supporting our clients and community.
2021
Our reach expanded nationwide as we continued growing our presence and connecting with individuals and families across the country.
2022
We expanded our men’s housing services with new locations in Westchester County and New York City.
2023
We launched our first clinical program and treatment space in Westchester County, marking an important step toward becoming a comprehensive behavioral healthcare treatment provider.
2024
We acquired MANUAL Care and rebranded it as Release U — a specialized resource designed to support the mental health and wellness needs of college students.
2025
We expanded into the South through the acquisition of a treatment program in Austin, while continuing to grow throughout New York with the launch of a women’s treatment program in Westchester County. We also expanded our clinical footprint in Westchester County by an additional 8,000 square feet, allowing us to serve more individuals struggling with mental health and substance use disorders.
Today
Today, we are a full-service behavioral healthcare organization with more than 120 employees, 90 beds across multiple programs, a mental health app serving over 30 colleges nationwide, and two treatment programs dedicated to helping individuals build lasting recovery and healthier futures.

FAQs

Who Will Help a Loved One or Myself Move Through the Process of Care?
All Release Recovery clients will be assigned a case manager. Case managers are recovery coaches that act as the “quarterback” of care. They meet with clients regularly, provide accountability and frequently assess what areas the client needs more support, ensure the clinical team, family, residential staff and important stakeholders receive the proper and timely communication, and provide start-to-end guidance and coordination of the treatment process. They can assist with clinical referrals, court date prep, recovery goals, securing employment, and more. Release is also proud to offer 24/7 support staff.
How Long Does the Admission Process Take?
Our team is largely home-grown, and the majority of us have lived experience. We understand first-hand the importance of urgency when a person is ready and willing to get help. We try to move as quickly through the process as possible, but it is comprehensive. To effectively capture all relevant information and history from current providers, loved ones, and the client themselves, ideally, we have at least one week from first contact to admission.

The Process

Integrated Recovery

At Release, the residential and clinical components are intentionally integrated to create a seamless, immersive treatment experience in both Westchester and Austin. Clients are supported within a structured living environment that reinforces consistency, accountability, and community, while simultaneously engaging in a robust clinical schedule. Rather than functioning as separate tracks, housing and treatment operate as one cohesive system where real-time experiences in the residence become material for clinical work, and therapeutic insights are immediately practiced in daily living.

This allows clients to build and apply coping skills in the environments where challenges naturally arise, fostering deeper insight, stronger peer connection, and more sustainable behavioral change. The result is a dynamic, recovery-oriented model that delivers the intensity and support of residential care while promoting autonomy, responsibility, and long-term readiness for life beyond treatment.

b. Use of Cookies

Release Recovery, Inc. may use cookies to personalize or enhance your user experience. A cookie is a small text file that is placed on your hard disk by a Web page server. Cookies cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer. Cookies are uniquely assigned to you, and can only be read by a Web Server in the domain that issued the cookie to you.

One of the primary purposes of cookies is to provide a convenience feature to save you time. For example, if you personalize a web page, or navigate within a site, a cookie helps the site to recall your specific information on subsequent visits. Hence, this simplifies the process of delivering relevant content and eases site navigation by providing and saving your preferences and login information as well as providing personalized functionality.

Release Recovery reserves the right to share aggregated site statistics with partner companies, but does not allow other companies to place cookies on our website unless there is a temporary, overriding customer value (such as merging into harmoni.io a site that relies on third-party cookies).

You have the ability to accept or decline cookies. Most Web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies. If you reject cookies by changing your browser settings then be aware that this may disable some of the functionality on our Website.

Our Clinical Programs are located in Westchester, New York and Austin, Texas. Our program offers a full continuum of clinical care, including individual and group therapy, evaluation, and medication management at every level.

Guided by a trauma-informed approach and overseen by our Chief Medical Officer, treatment is comprehensive, evidence-based, and tailored to each client’s unique needs throughout their recovery journey.

Method

Our Clinical Approach

It’s our belief that everyone coming into our care has some form of trauma. Therefore, our approach is centered around trauma-informed, evidence-based treatment that supports emotional, psychological, and physical healing. Our team integrates a wide range of therapies, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT), Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT), Motivational Interviewing (MI), Internal Family Systems (IFS), and the NeuroAffective Relational Model (NARM®).

All clients will work with our Chief Medical Officer, Dr. Michael McCormick, in addition to our team of Master’s Level Clinicians. Our variety of specialized groups, combined with our gender-specific, trauma informed approach, creates a safe space for all to recover.

Trauma in its purest form is defined as “Something that has happened to you that shouldn’t have or something that should have happened that didn’t.”
Clinical Programs

Our Continuum of Care

Release offers multiple levels of support for substance use (formerly known as addiction) and mental health. Whether you need more structure or are stepping down to less intensive care, our programs are designed to meet you where you are and help you move forward. Treatment is led by licensed clinicians in collaboration with our Chief Medical Officer to ensure personalized and comprehensive support.

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Release’s Partial Hospitalization Program offers structured treatment in a setting that supports flexibility and real-world transition. Clients have the option to live in our supportive housing. This program is ideal for those who need more structure than outpatient services can offer but do not require round-the-clock residential care.

  • 25 hours of group programming weekly
  • One individual session per week
  • Medical appointments and case management
  • Optional community housing with peer support
  • Breakfast and Lunch provided at Release Austin
  • Medication Management with Chief Medical Officer

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Release’s Intensive Outpatient Program provides more hours, more oversight, and more clinical support than traditional outpatient care. With a rotating 16-week curriculum, clients focus on emotional regulation, identity, boundaries, relapse prevention, and day-to-day life skills.

  • 12 hours of group therapy weekly (Mon–Thurs)
  • One individual session per week
  • Family therapy and psychiatric care as needed
  • Personalized support planning
  • Medication Management with Chief Medical Officer

Evening Intensive Outpatient Program

Release’s Evening Treatment Program is ideal for those transitioning back to work, school, or home life and want to stay connected to consistent support — such as working professionals, full time students, or busy parents.

  • Licensed clinicians & Full time Chief Medical Officer
  • 9 hours of group therapy weekly
  • Weekly individual session around your schedule
  • Family coordination & therapy as needed
  • Medication Management with Chief Medical Officer

Outpatient Program (OP)

Release’s Outpatient Program is ideal for clients who are ready for more independence while staying grounded in consistent care. It includes essential weekly touchpoints designed to maintain progress and prevent relapse.

  • Two group sessions per week
  • One individual session per week
  • Medical care as needed

FAQs

Everything You Need to Know

Who Will Help a Loved One or Myself Move Through the Process of Care?
All Release Recovery clients will be assigned a case manager. Case managers are recovery coaches that act as the “quarterback” of care. They meet with clients regularly, provide accountability and frequently assess what areas the client needs more support, ensure the clinical team, family, residential staff and important stakeholders receive the proper and timely communication, and provide start-to-end guidance and coordination of the treatment process. They can assist with clinical referrals, court date prep, recovery goals, securing employment, and more. Release is also proud to offer 24/7 support staff.
How Long Does the Admission Process Take?
Our team is largely home-grown, and the majority of us have lived experience. We understand first-hand the importance of urgency when a person is ready and willing to get help. We try to move as quickly through the process as possible, but it is comprehensive. To effectively capture all relevant information and history from current providers, loved ones, and the client themselves, ideally, we have at least one week from first contact to admission.
