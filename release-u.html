Release U

Release U offers evidence-based student well-being education, prevention, and support.

Release U is built on a simple belief: every student deserves access to well-being support. But as mental health demand continues to outpace what most institutions can provide alone, that belief has to be backed by real infrastructure. We partner with 80+ colleges, universities, and organizations nationwide to make scalable care a reality by bringing the tools, coaching, and resources students need directly to the people and places that serve them. Together we're expanding what's possible for student well-being.

Video Content
A library of on-demand video resources covering mental health, personal growth, and overall well-being, accessible anytime, anywhere. Students get consistent support between touchpoints; your institution gets measurable engagement.
1:1 Text Coaching
Personalized, real-time support that helps students navigate everyday challenges, build healthy habits, and stay accountable. A meaningful support layer that complements, and reduces pressure on, your existing staff.
Training Programs
Use our ready-to-go programs for compliance with federal and state regulations, or create your own custom program on our learning management system.
Keynotes & Workshops

Release U brings learning to life through interactive workshops and expert-led speaker sessions designed to meet students where they are, and give them real tools to navigate what they're actually facing.

Keynotes & Workshops are available in-person and virtually, covering topics including:

  • Navigating Burnout
  • Hazing
  • Addictive Behaviors
  • Career Resiliency
  • Substance Misuse & Addiction
  • Gambling & Sports Betting
  • Healthy Relationships
  • Building Community
  • … And more!

Our keynotes and workshops feature industry leaders, practitioners, and Release U team members who bring credibility, lived experience, and candid conversation to communities nationwide. Our roster of topics and speakers continues to grow, and sessions are tailored to meet the specific needs of your campus community.
