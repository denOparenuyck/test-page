Comprehensive Clinical Care

What’s included
Comprehensive Clinical Care

Our program offers a full continuum of clinical care, including individual and group therapy, evaluation, and medication management at every level. Guided by a trauma-informed approach and overseen by our Chief Medical Officer, treatment is comprehensive, evidence-based, and tailored to each client’s unique needs throughout their recovery journey.

Clinical approach:
Trauma in its purest form is defined as “Something that has happened to you that shouldn’t have or something that should have happened that didn’t.”

It’s our belief that everyone coming into our care has some form of trauma. Therefore, our approach is centered around trauma-informed, evidence-based treatment that supports emotional, psychological, and physical healing. Our team integrates a wide range of therapies, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT), Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT), Motivational Interviewing (MI), Internal Family Systems (IFS), and the NeuroAffective Relational Model (NARM®).

All clients will work with our Chief Medical Officer, Dr. Michael McCormick, in addition to our team of Master’s Level Clinicians. Our variety of specialized groups, combined with our gender-specific, trauma informed approach, creates a safe space for all to recover.

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Release Recovery’s Partial Hospitalization Program offers structured treatment in a setting that supports flexibility and real-world transition. Clients attend daytime or evening clinical sessions and have the option to live in our supportive housing.

This program is ideal for those who need more structure than outpatient services can offer but do not require round-the-clock residential care.

  • 25 hours of group programming weekly
  • Weekly 1:1 therapy
  • Medical appointments and case management
  • Optional community housing with peer support
  • Breakfast and Lunch provided at the Release Recovery Centers
Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Our IOP provides more hours, more oversight, and more clinical support than traditional outpatient care. With a rotating 12-week curriculum, clients focus on emotional regulation, identity, boundaries, relapse prevention, and day-to-day life skills.

  • 12 hours of group therapy weekly (Mon–Thurs)
  • Weekly individual therapy
  • Family therapy and psychiatric care as needed
  • Personalized support planning
Evening Intensive Outpatient Program

Our trauma-informed evening program supports working professionals and clients with busy daytime schedules. The program includes 12 hours of structured care per week and is led by licensed clinicians in close collaboration with our Chief Medical Officer.

  • 9 hours of weekly evening programming (Mon, Tues, Thurs)
  • Weekly individual therapy
  • Medical care as needed
Outpatient Program (OP)

Our OP is ideal for clients who are ready for more independence while staying grounded in consistent care. It includes essential weekly touchpoints designed to maintain progress and prevent relapse.

  • Weekly individual therapy
  • One group session per week
  • Medical care as needed

Structured Transitional Living

With over 80 beds across New York and Texas our residential, transitional living environments spare no details. Our residential environments offer comfort and confidentiality so that our clients and families can embark on the healing journeys they deserve.

Housing and treatment operate as one cohesive system in Westchester, allowing clients to build and apply coping skills in the environments where challenges naturally arise, fostering deeper insight, stronger peer connection, and more sustainable behavioral change.

Family Involvement

What’s included
Family Involvement

Research shows that the identified client has a higher likelihood of achieving long-term recovery when family members are actively engaged in their own recovery process. As part of the program, family members learn their own recovery tools, receiving education, therapy, and coaching.

  • Family Therapy & Coaching
  • Virtual Support Groups
  • Onsite Family Workshops
  • Professional Referrals
The Process

Integrated Recovery

At Release, the residential and clinical components are intentionally integrated to create a seamless, immersive treatment experience in both Westchester and Austin. Clients are supported within a structured living environment that reinforces consistency, accountability, and community, while simultaneously engaging in a robust clinical schedule. Rather than functioning as separate tracks, housing and treatment operate as one cohesive system where real-time experiences in the residence become material for clinical work, and therapeutic insights are immediately practiced in daily living.

This allows clients to build and apply coping skills in the environments where challenges naturally arise, fostering deeper insight, stronger peer connection, and more sustainable behavioral change. The result is a dynamic, recovery-oriented model that delivers the intensity and support of residential care while promoting autonomy, responsibility, and long-term readiness for life beyond treatment.
