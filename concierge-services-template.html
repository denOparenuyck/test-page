Recovery Coaching

No two recovery journeys are the same, and your coaching experience shouldn’t be either. Rather than using a one-size-fits-all approach, we focus on your individual goals, strengths, and the areas where you want the most support. Together, we create a plan that feels motivating, realistic, and aligned with the life you want to build. Recovery is personal, and clients play an active role in shaping the direction of their journey while being supported every step of the way.

Our Mission

Personal Growth Through Recovery

To help clients achieve and maintain their personal recovery goals through non-clinical, experience-based guidance that encourages accountability, personal growth, and lasting positive change.

Our in-house Recovery Coach, Aidan McGrath, Aidan has been in personal recovery since 2022, initially as a Release client. Aidan joined the Release team as a Recovery Coach and Associate in 2024. In coaching it’s made clear that the physical, mental, and emotional benefits of movement are indisputable.
