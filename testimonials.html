“Prior to Release, I’d lost multiple apartments, my job, my cat, and my relationship with my family was suffering. Most of all, I had lost myself. It wasn’t my first time trying to get sober, and I didn’t know if I was ready to fully surrender. Even though I entered the program reluctantly, I quickly found that the structure and community would provide me with the support I needed to maintain long-term sobriety. The staff was patient and kind with me as I gained back my footing. As my willingness grew, my life did too.They taught me how to laugh again. They sat with me through the tears. During my 8-month stay, Release helped me find two jobs that I loved, an apartment that I’ve gotten to stay at, a group of lifelong friends, and a meaningful relationship with my family. I gained the sense of serenity and freedom that I’d been searching for in drugs all along. I gained more back than I’d ever lost and certainly more than I ever expected. Most importantly, I found myself again, and that is the greatest gift I could’ve asked for.”
