More Than Recovery

Education and prevention are at the heart of our broader mission to
help individuals, families, and communities thrive. From campuses
to living rooms, we create real-world tools that build resilience,
reduce stigma, and foster connection.

Care for Loved Ones

Family Education & Support

Guidance for the Journey

Guidance for the Journey

Recovery affects more than the individual. Our family programming helps loved ones feel informed, involved, and supported throughout the process.

We provide education, communication strategies, and expert guidance to help families process their own experience while also showing up for the person in treatment. Families are encouraged to take part in programming that builds empathy, sets boundaries, and fosters lasting recovery for everyone involved.

Weekly Family Support Group

Weekly Family Support Group

Release's weekly Family Support Group is led by our in-house Social Worker and Case Managers. These meetings serve as a safe space for Release Families to receive support & engage in our community. All family members are welcome to attend.

Quarterly Family Wellness Weekend

Quarterly Family Wellness Weekend

Held quarterly at our clinical facility in Westchester County, Release’s Family Wellness Weekend is a full weekend of connection, education, healing, and support for families navigating recovery together. Led by our world-class case managers, the experience features inspiring stories of hope from program alumni and their loved ones, as well as informative sessions with our expert clinicians and Chief Medical Officer.

The Recovery Conversation

The Zac Clark Show

The Zac Clark Show is a powerful resource for individuals, families, and
communities seeking honest conversations around addiction, mental health,
and recovery. Hosted by Zac Clark — who knows addiction from both sides,
as a former heroin addict who rebuilt his life and as Founder and CEO of
Release Recovery — the podcast challenges stigma and reshapes the
conversation around recovery. Each week, behavioral healthcare experts,
public figures, and everyday people join Zac to share real stories, practical
tools, and hope for those navigating substance use and mental health
challenges, offering proof that recovery is possible.
The podcast is a storytelling platform that opens the door to understanding,
whether you're in recovery, supporting someone who is, or simply curious
about the human experience.
