Healing Environments Designed for Recovery

All of our properties are designed with intention. Each location combines the highest standard of clinical care with a restorative setting that feels welcoming and supportive. Medical and therapeutic needs are met in spaces that foster trust, comfort, and a sense of belonging.

Every property supports the full continuum of care, blending structured daily schedules with therapy, peer support, and wellness activities. Amenities are selected not only for quality but for their role in building skills, resilience, and independence for lasting recovery.

Supportive Living Environments

Release Recovery’s transitional living programs set the standard, offering structured, supportive environments designed to promote lasting recovery. With three residences in New York City, three in Westchester County, and one in Austin, Texas, each property is intentionally designed to provide a restorative, welcoming environment that fosters comfort, trust, and a true sense of belonging.

Structured Transitional Living

*Our transitional living program in NYC is not covered by insurance and requires private pay.

Since 2017, our founder has operated transitional living residences, making this our legacy service and the foundation on which Release Recovery was built. While our organization has grown and evolved, our commitment to providing best-in-class transitional living has remained unchanged.

Our program provides structure, accountability, and community support to foster recovery, build vocational and life skills, and promote lasting self-sufficiency. Each client works with a dedicated case manager to develop a personalized treatment plan that reflects their individual recovery, personal, and professional goals.

Our program offers:

  • 24/7 Trained Staff
  • Daily Community Programming
  • Transportation
  • Clinical Collaboration
  • Personalized Recovery Tracks
  • In-House Chef
  • Case Management Services
  • Money Management: True Link
  • Observed Drug Testing & Breathalyzing
  • Family Programming
  • Life Skill Development & Vocational Support
