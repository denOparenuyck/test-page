Healing Environments Designed for Recovery

All of our properties are designed with intention. Each location combines the highest standard of clinical care with a restorative setting that feels welcoming and supportive. Medical and therapeutic needs are met in spaces that foster trust, comfort, and a sense of belonging.

Every property supports the full continuum of care, blending structured daily schedules with therapy, peer support, and wellness activities. Amenities are selected not only for quality but for their role in building skills, resilience, and independence for lasting recovery.

Our Residences

Supportive Living Environments

Release Recovery’s transitional living programs set the standard, offering structured, supportive environments designed to promote lasting recovery. With three residences in New York City, three in Westchester County, and one in Austin, Texas, each property is intentionally designed to provide a restorative, welcoming environment that fosters comfort, trust, and a true sense of belonging.

New York City Men’s Transitional Living

We offer two luxury apartments in Manhattan—one located in the vibrant Flatiron district and the other in the charming heart of Greenwich Village. Both feature private outdoor spaces and are conveniently situated near subway stops, offering easy access to the city’s best attractions. New York City is home to one of the best and most robust recovery communities in the country.

New York City Women’s Transitional Living

Located in a five-story brownstone on Manhattan’s Upper East Side, Release Recovery’s Women’s Program offers a safe, warm environment for women navigating addiction and mental health challenges. Our residence combines the comfort of home with access to world-class recovery resources and amenities, including 24/7 in-house support staff and an in-house chef.

Westchester Men’s Transitional Living

Our Westchester men’s residences offer structured, supportive living just outside the city—designed for comfort, accountability, and connection. Clients benefit from a restorative setting paired with access to Release’s clinical campus and a full continuum of care.

Westchester Women’s Transitional Living

The Westchester women’s program provides a warm, intentional living environment where residents can focus on recovery while building independence. Thoughtfully designed spaces and nearby clinical support create a foundation for lasting change.

Release Austin Transitional Living

Release Austin’s transitional living residence offers a calm, supportive home base for clients in recovery. The property is designed to foster comfort and belonging while connecting residents to clinical care, community, and daily structure that promotes lasting self-sufficiency.

Release Recovery Centers

Our thoughtfully designed 16,000-square-foot facility supports every stage of the recovery journey, featuring welcoming group rooms, private therapy offices, gender-specific spaces, an on-site doctor’s office and nurse station, a cafeteria, and a comfortable client lounge that fosters connection, reflection, and healing.

Release Austin Clinic

Release Austin’s clinic provides a welcoming, client-centered space for outpatient care—designed to support connection, comfort, and personalized treatment throughout every stage of the recovery journey.
